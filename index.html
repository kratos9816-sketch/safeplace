Constellation IV • The Vulnerable Truth

The Silent Fear of Losing You

Behind every smile and every tender moment, there is a quiet panic inside my heart — because without you, I am completely lost in the dark.

01 • The Daily Quiet Panic

"What if one day, you decide to walk away?"

I don't think you truly understand how terrified I am of losing you. In the middle of an ordinary day, in random quiet moments when the world pauses, a suffocating whisper creeps into my chest: "What if she leaves? What if one day she realizes she doesn't want me anymore?" It makes my hands cold. It makes my heart race. I try to act strong and calm on the outside, but the mere thought of waking up in a world where Kirthikha isn't mine leaves me feeling like a lost child with nowhere to go.

"Enaku eppome, ella neramum oru chinna bayam irukummm... Nee enna vendam nu solliduviyaoo, enna vittu poiduviyaoo nu..."
02 • You Are My Only Quiet Place

"My mind never stops screaming... except in your arms."

I am an overthinker. My brain constantly runs at a hundred miles an hour, analyzing every fear, every flaw, every doubt. But the moment you are next to me? Complete silence. Mind la oru sound kooda irukathu. The night I rested my head on your chest and fell asleep was the deepest peace I have ever known in my entire existence. You are not just someone I love — you are the only medicine that calms my soul. If you ever leave, that silence disappears, and I am drowned in chaos forever.

"Anaaa nee en pakathula iruntha mattum pothummm, enaku avlooooo amaithiya irukummm."
03 • Loving You With No Armor

"You hold the power to destroy me with a single goodbye."

Nejama soldren ma, you're a part of me. When you smile, I feel alive. When you are sad, my whole day falls apart. I have given you every key to my heart, leaving zero defense, zero walls, and zero protection. Loving you this deeply is the sweetest blessing of my life, but it is also the most terrifying thing I have ever done — because if you walk away, you don't just break my heart; you take away my identity, my peace, and my reason to smile.

"I can feel what you feel... Nee thaan enoda innoru half maaaa. ❤️"
04 • My Only Plea To Your Heart

"Promise me we will fight for each other, always."

I don't need a grand fairytale. I don't care about anything material in this universe. I just want to protect you, pamper you when you are tired, cook together in our silly kitchen, and fall asleep on your chest every single night. When days get hard, when we get angry, when we disagree — scream at me, scold me, cry with me, but please never let go of my hand. We will fix whatever breaks, as long as you stay right by my side.

"Endha avasaramum illaama namaloda azhgaana world la namma rendu perum happy ah irukalaammmm. ❤️"
